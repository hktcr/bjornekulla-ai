# WORKSHOP GEM PACKAGE - README

## 📦 What This Package Contains

This package contains all the materials needed to create a **Workshop Facilitator Gem** in Google Gemini that guides participants through the "FROM AUDIENCE TO CONDUCTOR" workshop.

## 📚 Files Included

1. **01_WORKSHOP_OVERVIEW_AND_PURPOSE.md** - Workshop philosophy, structure, goals, and key concepts
2. **02_COMPLETE_MANUSCRIPTS.md** - Voice-over scripts for all 5 films
3. **03_ALL_PROMPTS.md** - All 16 prompts with expected outcomes and troubleshooting
4. **04_PAUSE_AND_TASK_ACTIVITIES.md** - All hands-on exercises between films
5. **05_SEMINAR_QUESTIONS.md** - All reflection and discussion questions
6. **06_FACILITATOR_INSTRUCTIONS.md** - Instructions for how the Gem should facilitate the workshop
7. **07_EXAMPLE_CONTENT.md** - Example content for exercises (assessment items, style guide, Gem template, code, emails)
8. **08_TECHNICAL_SETUP.md** - Step-by-step instructions for accessing Gemini, NotebookLM, and Gem Manager
9. **09_PROMPTS_FOR_COPY_PASTE.md** - All prompts in copy-paste format (no context, just prompts)
10. **10_ABOUT_THE_FILMS.md** - Information about films and how to use workshop without them
11. **README.md** - This file

## 🚀 How to Create the Workshop Facilitator Gem

### Step 1: Go to Google Gemini
1. Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
2. Click on "Gem manager" (top right)
3. Click "Create new Gem"

### Step 2: Configure the Gem

**Name:** Workshop Facilitator - From Audience to Conductor

**Description:** Guides participants through a 2.5-3 hour workshop on AI orchestration using the Three Roles framework. Provides prompts, instructions, and reflection questions at the right time.

**Instructions:** (Paste the content from **06_FACILITATOR_INSTRUCTIONS.md**)

### Step 3: Upload Knowledge Base

Upload these files as "Knowledge" for the Gem:
- 01_WORKSHOP_OVERVIEW_AND_PURPOSE.md
- 02_COMPLETE_MANUSCRIPTS.md
- 03_ALL_PROMPTS.md
- 04_PAUSE_AND_TASK_ACTIVITIES.md
- 05_SEMINAR_QUESTIONS.md
- 07_EXAMPLE_CONTENT.md
- 08_TECHNICAL_SETUP.md
- 09_PROMPTS_FOR_COPY_PASTE.md
- 10_ABOUT_THE_FILMS.md

**Note:** Do NOT upload 06_FACILITATOR_INSTRUCTIONS.md as knowledge - that content should be in the "Instructions" field.

### Step 4: Test the Gem

Start a conversation with the Gem and say:

"Hi, I'm [your role] and I'd like to go through the workshop."

The Gem should:
1. Welcome you
2. Ask about your role and goals
3. Provide context for Film 1
4. Guide you through exercises
5. Adapt examples to your role

### Step 5: Save and Share

Once tested, save the Gem. You can now:
- Use it yourself to go through the workshop
- Share it with colleagues
- Use it to facilitate workshops

## 🎯 How to Use the Gem

### For Self-Paced Workshop

1. Open the Gem in Gemini
2. Say: "I'd like to start the workshop"
3. Follow the Gem's guidance through all 5 films and exercises
4. Complete hands-on exercises with YOUR OWN content
5. Participate in seminar reflection at the end

### For Facilitated Workshop

1. Open the Gem before the workshop
2. Use it as a co-facilitator during the workshop
3. Ask it for prompts, troubleshooting, or seminar questions
4. Let it adapt examples to participants' roles

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

### Do:
- ✅ Use YOUR OWN content in exercises (not generic examples)
- ✅ Complete all hands-on exercises
- ✅ Participate fully in seminar reflection
- ✅ Ask the Gem questions when you get stuck
- ✅ Take your time (2.5-3 hours is optimal)

### Don't:
- ❌ Skip exercises
- ❌ Use generic examples instead of your own content
- ❌ Rush through the workshop
- ❌ Skip the seminar (that's where transformation happens)
