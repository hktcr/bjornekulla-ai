## FILM 1: What is a virtual expert panel?

### Pause & Task
**Title:** Pause & Task: Create your first expert panel

**Task:**
Create your own virtual expert panel for a task relevant to YOUR role:

**Examples by role:**
- **Developers:** Code review, architecture decisions, debugging complex issues
- **Leadership:** Strategic decisions, risk analysis, stakeholder communication
- **Content creators:** Writing, editing, quality assurance
- **Designers:** UX feedback, accessibility review, design critique
- **Support:** Troubleshooting guides, FAQ creation, customer communication
- **Sales/Marketing:** Pitch decks, customer emails, campaign copy
- **Communication:** Press releases, internal announcements, crisis communication

**Steps:**
1. Open Gemini (gemini.google.com)
2. Think of a task from your work where you need feedback
3. Use this prompt template (adapt [YOUR AREA] and [TYPE OF CONTENT]):

```
Assemble a panel of 10 experts in [YOUR AREA] to review [TYPE OF CONTENT].

Coordinator:
You are a professional facilitator for this expert panel.
After the panel is assembled, ask the user:
"How would you like to proceed?"
A) Hear individual opinions from each expert
B) Have the panel discuss together
C) Request a synthesis
D) Something else (please specify)

After each completed step, ask: "What would you like to do next?" and offer context-appropriate options.
Never proceed without explicit user direction.
```

4. Paste your adapted prompt into Gemini and press Enter
5. Read through the 10 experts that Gemini suggests
6. **STOP HERE** - don't answer the coordinator's question yet (we'll do that in Film 2)

**Write down:**
- What experts did Gemini suggest?
- Are any important perspectives missing?
- How long did it take to create the panel?

**Time:** 10 minutes


---


### Seminar Questions

**Experience:**
1. What area did you create your expert panel for? (Assessment, code, strategy, content, design, support, sales, communication, other?)
2. What experts did Gemini suggest? Were you surprised by any of them?
3. How long did it take to create the panel? Faster or slower than expected?

**Quality of Experts:**
4. Did the panel include all the perspectives you need? What was missing (if anything)?
5. How do these AI experts compare to real colleagues you'd normally ask for feedback?
6. Would you trust this panel to review your work? Why or why not?

**Three Roles Understanding:**
7. In your panel, who are the **Experts** (👥)? What perspectives do they bring?
8. What did the **Coordinator** (🎯) do after creating the panel?
9. As the **Conductor** (👤), how does it feel to have the coordinator ask YOU how to proceed?

**Mindset Shift:**
10. Before this workshop, how did you think about AI tools? (Helper? Replacement? Threat? Tool?)
11. How does the "conductor" metaphor change your perspective?
12. What's the difference between "asking AI a question" and "conducting an expert panel"?

**Control:**
13. How does it feel to have the coordinator wait for your direction instead of just giving you answers?
14. Can you see yourself using this approach in your daily work? What would you use it for?

## FILM 2: Create your first expert panel in Gemini

### Pause & Task
**Title:** Pause & Task: Create your panel and test with YOUR content

**Task:**
Create your own expert panel in Gemini for a task relevant to YOUR role:

**Examples by role:**
- **Developers:** "Create a panel of 10 software architecture experts to review my API design"
- **Leadership:** "Create a panel of 10 organizational change experts to discuss my restructuring proposal"
- **Content creators:** "Create a panel of 10 instructional design experts to review my course outline"
- **Designers:** "Create a panel of 10 UX experts to critique my user flow"
- **Support:** "Create a panel of 10 customer service experts to review my troubleshooting guide"
- **Sales:** "Create a panel of 10 B2B sales experts to review my pitch deck"
- **Communication:** "Create a panel of 10 PR experts to review my press release"

**Steps:**
1. Open Gemini
2. Use the coordinator prompt (adapt the example to your domain)
3. Create your panel (10-15 minutes)
4. Test it with real content from your work (10-15 minutes)
5. Try different interaction modes (individual opinions, discussion, synthesis)

**Write down:**
- What worked well?
- What surprised you?
- How long did it take compared to your usual process?

**Time:** 25 minutes

---

### Seminar Questions

**Experience:**

2. How long did it take to create the panel? Was it faster or slower than expected?
3. What content did you test it with? (Code, strategy doc, course material, design, guide, email, etc.)

**Quality:**
4. How was the quality of feedback compared to what you usually get?
5. Did the panel identify issues you hadn't thought of?
6. Were there any "blind spots" the panel missed?

**Interaction:**
7. Which interaction mode worked best for you? (Individual opinions, discussion, synthesis)
8. How did the coordinator role affect the process?
9. Did you feel in control? What would you change?

**ROI:**
10. How long did the panel review take vs. your usual process?
11. If you used this weekly, how much time would you save?
12. What would you do with that saved time?

## FILM 3: Get interviewed by Gemini for your style document

### Pause & Task
**Title:** Pause & Task: Create YOUR style document

**Task:**
Let Gemini interview you to create a style document for YOUR domain:

**Examples by role:**
- **Developers:** Code style, documentation preferences, review criteria
- **Leadership:** Communication style, decision-making frameworks, leadership principles
- **Content creators:** Writing voice, pedagogical approach, quality standards
- **Designers:** Design principles, aesthetic preferences, UX philosophy
- **Support:** Tone of voice, troubleshooting approach, customer interaction style
- **Sales:** Pitch style, objection handling, relationship-building approach
- **Communication:** Brand voice, messaging framework, crisis communication principles

**Steps:**
1. Use the interview prompt (adapt to your domain)
2. Answer 8-10 questions honestly (15-20 minutes)
3. Review the generated style document
4. Save it (you'll use it in Film 4 and 5)

**IMPORTANT:** Save this document - you'll need it for the next two films!

**Time:** 20 minutes

---

### Seminar Questions

**Experience:**
1. What domain did you create your style document for?
2. How did it feel to be interviewed by AI? Weird? Natural? Helpful?
3. Did the interview questions help you articulate things you hadn't put into words before?

**Quality:**
4. Does the style document accurately capture YOUR voice/approach/principles?
5. What did it get right? What did it miss?
6. Would you have been able to write this document yourself? How long would it have taken?

**Insight:**
7. Did the interview help you discover or clarify your own preferences?
8. Were there questions that made you think differently about your work?
9. Did you learn something about yourself?

**Application:**
10. How will you use this style document? (Gem, NotebookLM, team onboarding, personal reference?)
11. Who else in your team/organization could benefit from creating their own style document?
12. What would happen if everyone in your team had their style documented?

## FILM 5: Expert panel in NotebookLM with sources

### Pause & Task
**Title:** Pause & Task: Create YOUR NotebookLM expert panel

**Task:**
Create a source-based expert panel in NotebookLM for YOUR domain:

**Examples by role:**
- **Developers:** Upload API docs, best practices guides, your code style → review code against standards
- **Leadership:** Upload company strategy, industry reports, your leadership principles → review decisions
- **Content creators:** Upload curriculum standards, research papers, your style guide → review content
- **Designers:** Upload design systems, accessibility guidelines, your design philosophy → review designs
- **Support:** Upload product docs, support best practices, your tone guide → review support content
- **Sales:** Upload sales methodology, customer case studies, your pitch style → review proposals
- **Communication:** Upload brand guidelines, crisis communication frameworks, your brand voice → review messages

**Steps:**
1. Open NotebookLM
2. Create new notebook
3. Upload 3-5 relevant sources (PDFs, docs, links)
4. Use the expert panel prompt (adapted to your domain)
5. Test with real content (15 minutes)
6. Check citations - do they point to your sources?

**Write down:**

- How much time will this save you?

## FINAL SEMINAR: Synthesis & Reflection

### Opening Questions (10 minutes)
1. Quick round: What's ONE thing you'll start using tomorrow?
2. What surprised you most about this workshop?

### Three Roles Deep Dive (15 minutes)
3. How has the "Three Roles" concept changed how you think about AI?
4. In your daily work, when do you need **Experts** (👥)? **Coordinator** (🎯)? When do you need to be **Conductor** (👤)?
5. What's the difference between "using AI" and "conducting AI"?

### Human Role (15 minutes)
6. **The big question:** What's YOUR role as a human now that you can conduct expert panels?
7. What can you do that AI cannot? (Even with perfect prompts and sources?)
8. How does your value change when you have access to expert panels?
9. Are you more valuable or less valuable? Why?

### Organizational Impact (15 minutes)
10. If everyone in your organization could conduct expert panels, what would change?
11. What new possibilities open up?
12. What risks or challenges do you foresee?
13. How would you introduce this to your team/organization?

### Personal Transformation (15 minutes)
14. How much time will you save per week using these tools?
15. What will you do with that time? (More work? Different work? Better work?)
16. How does this change your career trajectory?
17. What skills become more important? What skills become less important?

### Final Reflection (10 minutes)
18. Complete this sentence: "Before this workshop, I thought AI was ___. Now I think AI is ___."
19. What's the most important insight you're taking away?
20. What's your next step?
